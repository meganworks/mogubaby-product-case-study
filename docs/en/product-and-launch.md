## Positioning around a daily need

An early messaging approach emphasized childcare-entry preparation. Feedback revealed a more frequent job: **use existing food history to decide what to offer next**. Product messaging was therefore reorganized as a progression:

**Easy food logging → Useful history for everyday choices → Childcare preparation**

Milestone sharing and the family diary remained supporting emotional benefits rather than competing with the core functional promise.

## Scope and integrity of claims

The marketing iteration emphasized real workflows rather than portraying hypothetical features as delivered. In particular:

- Last-intake timing and sorting were described as **information for decisions**, not a verified improvement in nutritional balance.
- The childcare food list was not described as automatically assembled from any photograph.
- A user-facing claim was included only if it matched the app behavior scheduled for release.

## Release coordination

The revised five-image App Store presentation, store metadata, and a small food-history enhancement were aligned for version **1.0.1**. The project record documents final human approval, App Store submission, and publication in October 2026.

No verified causal uplift in downloads, conversion rate, or retention is claimed.
